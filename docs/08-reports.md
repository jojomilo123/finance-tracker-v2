# Finance Tracker SaaS

## Part 8 — Reports, Analytics & Financial Intelligence

# Objective

Build a professional financial analytics platform.

This is NOT just a reports page.

The analytics should help users understand:

* Where their money goes
* Why it goes there
* How spending changes over time
* How to improve their financial habits
* Whether they are reaching their financial goals

The experience should feel comparable to:

* Copilot Money
* Monarch Money
* Power BI
* Tableau
* Google Analytics
* Apple Health Trends

The application should generate insights instead of merely displaying raw numbers.

---

# Analytics Philosophy

Every chart must answer a question.

Examples:

Where am I spending the most?

Why are my expenses increasing?

Am I improving compared to last month?

Which category wastes the most money?

How healthy are my finances?

Every visualization must provide context.

Never display charts without explanation.
