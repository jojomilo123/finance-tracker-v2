# Finance Tracker SaaS

## Part 9 — Premium Features, AI & Smart Financial Platform

# Objective

Transform the application from a traditional expense tracker into an intelligent financial platform.

The application should become a financial assistant.

Not merely software.

The goal is to help users make better financial decisions automatically.

---

# AI Financial Assistant

Create an AI assistant that understands the user's financial data.

The assistant should answer questions naturally.

Examples:

Where did my money go this month?

How much did I spend on food?

What is my biggest expense?

Am I saving enough?

What can I reduce?

How much money can I safely spend this weekend?

How much will I have at the end of this month?

Which subscriptions should I cancel?

Which merchant receives the most money?

How much do I spend eating outside?

Compare this month with last month.

Summarize my financial condition.
